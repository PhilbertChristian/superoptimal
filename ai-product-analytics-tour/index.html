Come prepared

This is a hands-on evening, so please bring:

  • A laptop and an app with an AI feature you can run locally
  • No app with you? You'll work on the sample app

What you take home

  • Your AI feature, instrumented: events, a funnel and traces on your own code
  • An eval loop you can rerun: a judge prompt, a score and a chart
  • A reference event schema: the one used at HeadOn, ready to adapt
  • The session recording, sent after your city

Taught on a real product

Most AI features look fine in the demo and fail quietly in production. Adam walks through the analytics behind HeadOn, an AI product with real users, then you build the same setup on your own app. Same workshop, same lecturer, in every city.

Who should join

AI engineers, product engineers and founding engineers shipping agents, copilots, retrieval or summarization, and anyone asked to show that an AI feature is worth what it costs.

Lecturer Adam Becker
Adam Boaz Becker

CEO of HeadOn, and the person running the room.

Adam studied astrophysics at Berkeley and spent years in the machine learning community before building HeadOn, an AI-powered platform for hard political conversations. Every example in this workshop comes from instrumenting HeadOn's AI features with real users on the other side.

The tour grows out of the rooms The Superoptimal ran this fall in Chicago, Seattle, Barcelona and San Francisco. Now the same format goes to eleven cities, with Adam in every one.

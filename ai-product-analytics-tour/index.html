AI Product Analytics Tour

How do you know your AI product is right? A hands-on workshop taught by Adam Becker, on tour in 11 cities from December 2026 to April 2027.

11 events Taught by Adam Becker 3 hours, hands-on

About the tour

Most AI features look fine in the demo and fail quietly in production. This workshop teaches you to catch that early: whether users finish what the AI started, what each answer costs, and whether the answer was any good.

Adam walks through the analytics behind HeadOn, an AI product with real users, then you build the same setup on your own app: product events, a funnel, LLM traces and an eval loop you can rerun.

Same workshop, same instructor, in every city.
